About me

I'm Chukwunonye Ejekam, a final-year Computer Science student at Pan-Atlantic University who builds frontend web applications.

Most of my projects start from something I've seen people struggle with — finding someone trustworthy to share rent with, or ordering groceries from a nearby shop and actually knowing when the rider will arrive.

I care about interfaces that are fast on mid-range phones, readable in sunlight, and honest about what's happening behind the scenes. Right now I'm going deeper into React, state management and deploying real products.
